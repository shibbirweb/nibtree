# Overlays components

Generated by `bun run ui:docs`. Back to the [index](../../COMPONENTS.md).

- [Dialog](../components/Dialog.md): A modal dialog over a dimmed window: a title, the content and the actions on the
  right.
- [Popover](../components/Popover.md): A panel anchored to its trigger, like the AI usage panel; Esc or an outside click
  closes it.
- [Menu](../components/Menu.md): A context or drop-down menu at a point, with hints, separators and submenus; keyboard
  friendly.
- [MenuBar](../components/MenuBar.md): A bar of menus under their names, like a window's File, Edit and View.
- [MenuButton](../components/MenuButton.md): A button that opens a Menu under itself, like a "..." button.
- [ToastHost](../components/ToastHost.md): Draws toasts as Notification balloons in the bottom right corner, above the
  status bar and beside the right activity bar.
- [WindowLayer](../components/WindowLayer.md): A modal page over the whole window, with no dimming or frame; its content
  fills it as a column.
- [LoadingOverlay](../components/LoadingOverlay.md): A card over a dimmed window while something long happens: spinner,
  title, step and hint.
- [CommandPalette](../components/CommandPalette.md): A floating list at the top of the window to find and run something,
  like Go to File.
- [ChoiceMenu](../components/ChoiceMenu.md): A menu of one-of choices in a Popover: a title with its status, then radio
  choices and descriptions.
- [DialogHead](../components/DialogHead.md): A dialog's head with more than a title: an icon on a tinted tile, a
  subtitle and a close button.
- [PaletteRow](../components/PaletteRow.md): One row of a CommandPalette: icon or badge, title with matches, then check,
  reason, folder and keys.
- [CrumbPopup](../components/CrumbPopup.md): The list under a breadcrumb: a filter field, then the folder's entries in
  their git colors.
