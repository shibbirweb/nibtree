# Getting help

- **How do I ...?** The [user guide](https://github.com/shibbirweb/nibtree/wiki) covers every feature with
  screenshots. [Troubleshooting](https://github.com/shibbirweb/nibtree/wiki/Troubleshooting) and
  [Troubleshooting Git](https://github.com/shibbirweb/nibtree/wiki/Troubleshooting-Git) answer the common problems.
- **Something is broken.** Check the
  [known issues](https://github.com/shibbirweb/nibtree/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22known+issue%22),
  then [report a bug](https://github.com/shibbirweb/nibtree/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Settings > About >
  Report a Bug in the app fills in your versions.
- **I want something new.** [Request a feature](https://github.com/shibbirweb/nibtree/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
  The [milestones](https://github.com/shibbirweb/nibtree/milestones) show what is planned.
- **What changed?** The [releases](https://github.com/shibbirweb/nibtree/releases) and
  [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md), also shown in the app under What's New.
- **A security problem.** Report it privately, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Nibtree is maintained in spare time, so answers can take a few days. A report with exact steps and versions gets
fixed fastest.
